Hailing from the heart of IL, Missy Midwest is a resident DJ, music producer, and vocalist delivering divinely feminine energy, big hip-hop attitude, and unapologetic bad bitch bass.

With residencies in Lake of the Ozarks, MO & on the island of St. Croix, she brings feminine energy and versatility to every performance. She seamlessly blends genres like UKG, house, hip-hop, drum & bass, pop, 140, country, afrobeats and timeless club classics to keep the dance floor alive.

Her journey has been picking up speed as she released her first self-produced EP entitled "Bad Bitch Bass Vol. 1" on Halloween of 2026. She’s now taken her brand offshore to the island of St. Croix to fulfill a DJ residency with the goal of adding "international" to her resume.
